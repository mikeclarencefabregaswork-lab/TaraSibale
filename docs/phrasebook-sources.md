# Asi phrasebook sources

The former two demo entries were unsupported. Migration 007 archives them and publishes five short expressions checked against the Asi column of [Tagmi’s scanned phrasebook](https://www.tagmi.net/en/everyday-expressions-phrase-book).

The book is *Everyday Conversation: A Phrase Book for Bantoanon (Asi)*, Summer Institute of Linguistics, 1985. Its preface credits a team including Gail Hendrickson and Heather Kilgour, alongside local contributors. It presents Asi and Hiligaynon on separate columns, followed by Filipino and English on the facing page. Do not confuse these columns.

The five entries are a small selection, not a transcription of the book. Full dialogues and scans remain on the publisher’s site. `Backend/seed/sourced-phrases.json` records provenance and the date checked. The canonical catalog and frontend reference data contain the same entries.

These are documented Bantoanon–Asi expressions, not a claim of present-day Sibale-specific validation. A Sibale speaker should review regional usage and spelling before that claim is made. No native-speaker recording was verified: invented phonetic guides and the browser’s generic speech synthesis were removed. Do not restore pronunciation audio without a verified recording and permission to use it.

The unsupported greeting was also removed from the curated chatbot’s canned welcome and fallback replies. Earlier migrations retain historical demo text; they must not be rewritten after application.
