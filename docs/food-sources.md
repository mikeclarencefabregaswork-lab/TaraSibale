# Island Delicacies: source and photographs

Checked 26 October 2026. [DA-SAAD’s report, 29 December 2025](https://saad.da.gov.ph/saad-assisted-farmer-groups-in-sibale-island-boost-income-thru-kadiwa-ng-pangulo/) documents SAMASA offering leche flan and the Kaagapay Organization selling coco jam and atchara at Sibale KADIWA events. These are shared Filipino foods with documented local production, not proof of exclusive island origin. No present menu, price, recipe or vendor opening schedule is claimed.

The photographs show the food types, not products from those Sibale producers. Each card labels this explicitly. Local files avoid dependence on remote image delivery. Photographs were downloaded without editing; the UI scales them to fit without cropping.
